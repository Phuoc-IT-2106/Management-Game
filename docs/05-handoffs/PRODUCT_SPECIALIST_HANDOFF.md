# Context

The project is a PC business strategy / management simulation.

It begins with a professional esports organization but may later expand toward a broader multi-division company involving sports, media, advertising, events, and other business activities.

The current design direction emphasizes:
- strategic trade-offs,
- interconnected consequences,
- adaptation,
- imperfect information,
- organizational complexity,
- emergent situations.

The first product should remain deliberately narrow.

---

# Specialist Objective

Transform the approved Discovery results into a clear Product Foundation.

Do not design the entire game.

Do not choose the technical architecture.

Do not write implementation code.

---

# Required Work

1. Define the primary player fantasy.

2. Define the player's authority and responsibility boundaries.

3. Produce the core product promise.

4. Refine the preliminary design pillars.

5. Define what the game is.

6. Define what the game is NOT.

7. Identify the most important recurring player decisions.

8. Define short-, medium-, and long-term decision horizons.

9. Draft the first core management loop.

10. Identify which simulation details should remain abstract.

11. Propose a narrow vertical-slice product boundary.

12. Identify unresolved questions that must be sent back to Project Director.

---

# Required Outputs

Create or propose updates for:

- GAME_VISION.md
- PRODUCT_BOUNDARIES.md
- PLAYER_ROLE.md
- CORE_DECISIONS.md
- CORE_LOOP.md

Do not silently modify approved decisions.

---

# Quality Test

The Product Foundation should make it possible to answer:

- Who is the player?
- What are they responsible for?
- What do they repeatedly decide?
- Why are those decisions difficult?
- What changes over time?
- What creates long-term motivation?
- What is deliberately outside the first product?

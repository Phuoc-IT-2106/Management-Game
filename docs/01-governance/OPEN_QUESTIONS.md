# OPEN QUESTIONS

These questions should be resolved progressively. Do not attempt to answer all of them immediately.

## Product
- What exact role does the player represent: owner, CEO, president, general manager, or an abstract company controller?
- Is the main fantasy about competitive success, company growth, wealth creation, empire building, or a balance of these?
- How simulation-heavy should the game be compared with accessibility and abstraction?

## Time
- What is the smallest meaningful simulation step?
- Does the game advance by day, week, event, or variable time?
- How long should one season or business year feel to the player?

## Company
- What decisions remain at company level?
- Which actions should eventually be delegatable to managers?
- How should organizational complexity increase as the company grows?
- Should expansion create administrative overhead and coordination costs?

## Economy
- What are the primary resources besides money?
- How should debt, investment, revenue, salary, infrastructure, and risk interact?
- How much financial detail is useful before it becomes bookkeeping?

## People
- How deep should individual player / employee simulation be?
- Which attributes are visible, estimated, or hidden?
- How important are personality, relationships, loyalty, ambition, stress, and leadership?

## Competition
- How deeply should esports matches be simulated?
- Does the player influence tactics or only organization-level preparation?
- Should multiple esports titles have distinct rule modules?

## Expansion
- What makes a new business division strategically different rather than simply another revenue source?
- Which systems should every division share?
- How should synergies work between sports, media, advertising, events, and other businesses?
- Should poor expansion create structural company risk?

## World Simulation
- How intelligent should competing organizations be?
- Which markets need simulation?
- How much should external events affect the player?
- Should trends, regulations, technology, or consumer preferences change?

## Failure & Success
- Can the company go bankrupt?
- Can the player be removed from leadership?
- Is there a formal victory condition?
- Is the game primarily sandbox-driven?

## Technical
- Which engine best fits a data-heavy PC management game?
- How much simulation needs to run when the player is not viewing a system?
- What needs deterministic simulation?
- How important are modding and user-generated content?

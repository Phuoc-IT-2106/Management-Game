# OPEN QUESTIONS — PHASE 1 REVIEW

Status: PROPOSAL — question triage for Director review  
Phase: Phase 1 — Product Foundation  
Authority: Review aid; `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` remains the supplied original

## Purpose

Group genuinely unresolved questions without losing their Discovery history. The completed Discovery worksheet gives user answers to the eight Phase 0 questions, and Phase 1 sources develop them. “Directionally answered” means these sources offer a direction; it does **not** mean a formal decision has been recorded.

## Product

- **Directionally answered, pending ratification:** company-first executive management is the product center; competitive results support the business. This narrows the original “competition vs company” and “how simulation-heavy” questions (`PRODUCT_BOUNDARIES.md` §2).
- **Open:** exact first validation boundary and success signal need Director approval (`PRODUCT_BOUNDARIES.md` §§5–6).

## Player Authority

- **Directionally answered, pending ratification:** executive plus management control with operational delegation; optional deeper competitive control (`PLAYER_ROLE.md` §2).
- **Open:** founder/owner-CEO, hired CEO, or abstract executive; possibility of dismissal; minimum manual decisions; staff recommendation visibility (`PLAYER_ROLE.md` §7).

## Core Loop

- **Proposed:** Observe → Prioritize → Decide → Commit → Delegate / Intervene → Advance Time → Resolve → Review → Adapt (`CORE_LOOP.md` §1).
- **Open for Phase 2:** decision triggers, immediate versus delayed consequences, and how the short, medium, and long loops interact in the minimum model (`CORE_LOOP.md` §8).

## Time

- **Open for Phase 2:** smallest meaningful step; day, week, event, or variable advance; length of a season or business year. No exact time model is approved (`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, `CORE_LOOP.md` §8).

## Resources / Economy

- **Open for Phase 2:** minimum resources beyond money; relationship among reputation, audience, and commercial value; useful finance granularity; distress and insolvency thresholds (`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, `CORE_DECISIONS.md` §7, Phase 1 handoff).

## People

- **Open:** first-slice staff roles; information quality and visibility; minimum useful individual depth; which personnel decisions can be delegated (`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, `PLAYER_ROLE.md` §7).

## Competition

- **Directionally answered, pending ratification:** competition matters as business leverage, and manual management may be optional (`PRODUCT_BOUNDARIES.md` §2).
- **Open for Phase 2:** minimum competitive decisions and conceptual match-resolution depth. Detailed tactics and multiple disciplines are outside the proposed first slice (`CORE_DECISIONS.md` §7, `PRODUCT_BOUNDARIES.md` §5).

## Rival Organizations

- **Directionally answered, pending ratification:** rivals should evolve and compete (`PRODUCT_BOUNDARIES.md` §2).
- **Open for Phase 2:** minimum rival ecosystem and which rival actions must affect player decisions (Phase 1 handoff, `CORE_LOOP.md` §8).

## Failure

- **Directionally answered, pending ratification:** structural failure and a recovery window are intended (`PRODUCT_BOUNDARIES.md` §2).
- **Open for Phase 2:** recoverable distress versus terminal insolvency, leadership removal, victory condition, and sandbox framing (`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, Phase 1 handoff).

## World Simulation

- **Open for later design:** which markets and external changes matter to the narrow product; how much regulation, technology, trends, and consumer preferences affect decisions (`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`).

## Expansion

- **Directionally answered, pending ratification:** company-first long-term identity with esports as the initial domain; broad expansion is future scope (`PRODUCT_BOUNDARIES.md` §§2, 7).
- **Open for later phases:** which new divisions create strategic value, how shared company concepts and synergies work, and what structural risk expansion creates (`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`).

## Technical — deferred

- Engine, UI framework, deterministic simulation, background simulation, modding, and technical persistence are deferred until technical requirements are validated (`ROADMAP.md` P4, `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`).

## Historical status note

They should be marked resolved or superseded in the canonical question list only after Director review.

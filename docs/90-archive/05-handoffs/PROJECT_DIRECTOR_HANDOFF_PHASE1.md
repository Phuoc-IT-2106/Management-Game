# HANDOFF TO PROJECT DIRECTOR — PHASE 1 PRODUCT FOUNDATION

## Outcome

A first Product Foundation has been drafted from the accepted Phase 0 directions.

Created:

- `PLAYER_ROLE.md`

- `CORE_DECISIONS.md`
- `CORE_LOOP.md`

The foundation keeps the strategic center at executive + management company leadership, with esports as the narrow validation domain.

## Decisions Confirmed

The documents treat the following handoff directions as accepted:

- player primarily acts as CEO / executive;
- executive + management are the main control layers;
- operational micromanagement is secondary;
- competitive performance is a major source of business leverage;
- competitive management can be manual or delegated;
- simulation is medium-depth by default with selective deep systems;
- rival organizations evolve and compete;
- structural failure is real, with recovery/restructuring before game over in most cases;
- long-term identity is company-first;
- early implementation remains narrow.

## Proposals

1. Core product promise:
   Lead an esports company through consequential executive and management decisions where competitive and business systems interact.

2. Authority boundary:
   Manual competitive control is optional depth; low-level operations are delegated or abstracted.

3. Decision model:
   Core decisions must contain opportunity cost, uncertainty, cross-system consequences, and context-dependent value.

4. Initial core loop:
   Observe → Prioritize → Decide → Commit → Delegate/Intervene → Advance Time → Resolve → Review → Adapt.

5. Vertical-slice boundary:
   One company, one discipline, one main team, limited staff, rivals, competition, contracts, finance, sponsorship, reputation/audience linkage, delegation, and recovery pressure.

6. GAME_VISION revision:
   Clarify current player role, authority, competition-as-business-leverage, simulation depth, and first-product scope.

## Decision quality

Purpose: accept a specific offer. Context: live company/function/day/checkpoint.
Information: actual terms/dates/eligibility. Trade-off: income versus load and a
limited slot. Uncertainty: known rate versus unknown realization, labelled forecast.
Commitment/reversibility: explicit final material action, no termination fiction.
Timing: scheduled income is not cash now; actual wins earn later bonuses.
Feedback: real response and refreshed state. Company connection: Finance and future
preparation conversion. Repeatability: one inline review and exact context return.

These are engineering improvements over concatenated sponsor text, not proof that
players understand or prefer the screen. Director review should ask a player to
explain why a larger payment can be costly, when cash arrives, what signing changes,
and where to return; compare with the retained internal tab.

Recommendation: **ITERATE FIRST CONTEXTUAL WORKSPACE**. Retain this live exemplar;
complete human/input review and address measured rebind/confirmation cost before
claiming production UX acceptance. No gameplay redesign or fallback is needed.

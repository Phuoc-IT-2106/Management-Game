# Playable scope

One company, one fictional 5v5 discipline, one primary team, six starting players,
one Head Coach, two compressed rivals and four fixtures within a 28-day development
campaign. Counts, denomination and numerical calibration are NONCANONICAL DEVELOPMENT
FIXTURE choices, not production content commitments. User approved fixture use.

Start/load → inspect dashboard/team/opponent/finance/sponsors → commit preparation,
posture and lineup or coach authority → sign/release a bounded player and/or accept
a sponsor → advance through daily phases → review competition and owner-applied
business consequences → adapt and repeat. End of fixture is a review boundary,
not campaign insolvency or an invented season-extension mechanic.

| Decision | Authority | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three-way preparation | DEC-018/019 | Execution vs opponent-specific vs meta work |
| Conservative/balanced/aggressive posture | DEC-019; competition spec §6 | Reliability vs upside; matchup and exposure |
| Legal lineup exception | DEC-019 | Strength vs readiness/role suitability |
| Coach manual/recommend/autonomous, risk ceiling | DEC-014 | Approval burden and bounded authority |
| Sign/release a contracted person | DEC-002; economy §9–11; product core decisions | Capability vs liquidity/future salary; release retains arrears |
| Accept sponsor terms | DEC-003/017; economy §7/9/10 | Scheduled income vs delivery load, finite opportunity |

No situational pivot, series simulation, facilities, deep staff/relationships,
merchandise, M&A, broad scouting, multiple teams/disciplines, final art/balance,
modding, multiplayer, online services or Phase 6 work.

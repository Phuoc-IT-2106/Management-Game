## Shared scope and comparison conditions

Both directions operate the same company, sole development esports branch/team,
finance/sponsor, roster/coach/preparation, affairs and results. Both preserve
Company → Competitive Portfolio → Esports → Development Discipline → Primary Team
and distinguish shared functions from owned businesses. Company, Business & Finance,
Competitive Portfolio, Talent & Performance and Affairs & Time are the supported
lenses. There are no empty future divisions, headquarters rooms or invented resources.

The common walkthrough is an available sponsor offer affecting current company
load and scheduled receipts while a real competition checkpoint approaches. Use
the same observed offer, scope, revision, current load/capacity and dates in each
direction; no cosmetic scenario advantage. Comparison also includes quiet company,
accepted agreement, actual result after advance, unavailable offer and long names.

**FACT:** sponsor entry/commit/return is live. Broader competition/talent/finance/
review screens work in internal Main, but their Company navigation integration is
proposed. Drawings show target composition of supported gameplay, not an implemented
new scene. R1–R8 refer to the shared traceability matrix. No illustration owns a fact.

**PROPOSAL:** use one local selection keyed by company/session, scope/matter/entity
and observed revision. A labelled structured navigator can be opened from any
Company state, with visible focus and the same targets as the center. Company
selection and Back remain explicit. Scope selection, inspection and date focus
never advance time or commit. New/load invalidates old context; missing targets
fall back with explanation. No raw IDs/revisions appear as product identity.

All wireframes below show semantic grouping and emphasis. They are not pixel
allocations, screen captures, final styling, or proof of rendered 720p usability.

## Direction A — Company operating field

### A. Concept statement

**PROPOSAL:** operate one visibly owned company whose capabilities support and
constrain its competitive business. The primary act is choosing an affected place
or relationship, then addressing its matter. Persistent company containment, actual
operating state and a selected consequence carry identity even without a logo.

This is a bounded schematic of the company. Ownership is shown by containment and
labelled ancestry; shared support is shown by labelled relationships across it.
There is no arbitrary node positioning, map travel, zoom prerequisite or graph editor.

### B. Composition and C. central visualization

At 1080p, the company enclosure occupies the main reading area. Its owned operating
field shows the sole team and current preparation/checkpoint context. Shared Business
& Finance and Talent & Performance are responsibility anchors along its boundary,
not equal module cards. Select a sponsor matter to emphasize the finance-to-team
constraint and its due receipts; select preparation to emphasize team/coach context.
An attached matter explanation answers why now and where to act. It does not become
a generic attribute inspector.

Center state changes for real reasons: plan committed/missing, next checkpoint,

health meter is needed. All relationships have the same text explanation in the
structured/contextual path. Full ancestry is accessible even when a single-child
path is compressed.

### D. Navigation

Company returns to root orientation. Portfolio/branch/team selects owned scope;
Business & Finance or Talent & Performance selects a shared lens on that scope.
The same matter can be reached at its attachment, through the bounded matter area,
or through Affairs. Enter opens its existing supported workspace with origin
retained. The Company field yields to the decision workspace rather than taking
half of its reading space. Back restores valid scope/matter/focus/scroll.

The structured navigator uses company/owned branch followed by separately labelled
functions; it can temporarily occupy reading space for keyboard navigation, but
closing it restores the meaningful company center. It does not become the default
giant tree. No new keyboard shortcuts are assumed. Tab/focus/activation and labelled
native controls follow existing contracts. Non-sponsor destinations require the
reviewed integration identified in traceability; current Main remains the working path.

### E. Situations and decisions

Sponsor matter appears at Business & Finance, with the affected team relationship
visible: why now, scope, actual deadline, scheduled receipts, load and unknown wins.
Selection changes orientation only. Enter → company/document context → terms and
actor-safe evidence → accept versus leave comparison → review → deliberate commitment
→ authoritative refreshed agreement/load/receivables → return to the same company
relationship. After existing advance, review refreshed cash/receipt balances and
actual results, then adapt preparation/roster/coach choices through supported paths.

Financial receipt inspection is read-only. It cannot masquerade as an accept or
collect-payment action. A financial/preparation blocker uses the actual Application
reason; an attractive sponsor offer is not automatically blocking. Quiet state keeps
real company structure, next checkpoint and normal commitments without fabricated urgency.

receipts, hidden competitive inputs and debugging records are excluded. Current
receipt balances do not imply a complete settlement history.

## Direction B — Company commitment horizon

pressure on the owned business. The primary act is selecting an actual dated
obligation, opportunity or checkpoint, then inspecting its affected company scope.
The same ownership/function model remains accessible; chronology is the dominant

and actual competition dates. Company and owned branch frame the horizon; labelled
finance and talent support explain the same team's constraints. The selected matter
appears inline at its actual date with current/conditional consequence text. It is
not a task planner: no movable tasks, completion checkboxes, staff assignment lanes,
editable dates, progress-to-success meter or artificial future company snapshot.

### D. Navigation

Select a labelled current matter or real-ID financial item to reveal its scope and
inline explanation; enter its supported workspace. The day track drawing itself
remains passive evidence. Separate EntityLabels provide keyboard/pointer targets
from payment/offer/bill/next-fixture IDs. Later competition dates without IDs remain
schedule marks, not fake interactive events. Functions filter/inspect relevance
without making a new state owner or losing company/root access.

### E. Situations and decisions

Sponsor deadline is a labelled situation on the horizon. Selection displays known
terms, receipt timing and supplied if-accepted load/forecast, clearly separated
from signed commitments. Enter → context/terms/evidence → comparison → review →
commitment → refresh; the accepted opportunity is now shown in signed context,
without immediate Cash. Existing advance can later produce settlement/result;
review refreshed cash/receipt balances and actual competitive results, then adapt
preparation or commercial choice. Due dates never stand in for actual payment
timestamps; neither direction promises a complete dated financial history from
the current reads.

A time-distant offer cannot disappear solely because it lies outside the displayed
window: current matters retain a bounded text route. Actual blockers remain visible
independently of date. Unknown date gets explicit text, never a fabricated position.
Quiet state shows supported ongoing obligations and next checkpoint; finished state
offers actual results instead of projecting a fictional next season.

## Recommendation and Director decisions

**RECOMMENDED DIRECTION — PROPOSAL: A, Company operating field.**

It most directly makes the company and its shared capabilities visible, aligns
with existing bounded scope/relationship semantics, and supports quiet state without
requiring a broad structured event projection. It has a more manageable 720p plan
than a chronology-led center. These are design/engineering judgments, not measured
player preference, visual approval or a new project decision.

Retain one idea from B: a bounded conditional capacity/receipt horizon when the
selected sponsor question warrants it, using DayTrack and text. Keep that instrument
secondary and contextual. Do not merge B's full-screen chronology, an always-visible
tree and every instrument into A. B remains a legitimate time-led alternative for
Director comparison, not rejected gameplay or erased evidence. A third diagram/
campus skin would add little distinct interaction value and is intentionally omitted.

**Director decision required before advancement:** select A, select B, or request a
specific revision as the composition thesis; confirm whether A's labelled navigator
disclosure and 720p stacked matter preserve the desired company identity; choose
the bounded next review scope (sponsor path first is recommended). This does not
approve new gameplay, final art, identity migration or broad Company BUILD.

**Material open questions:**

1. Does the schematic company enclosure communicate company operation without a
   building illustration, and how much relationship detail should remain visible?
2. Is the proposed navigator disclosure sufficiently discoverable for keyboard
   users without making the tree the company's visual identity?
3. Which selected consequence and instrument must remain above the 720p fold?
   Sponsor critical load/timing/unknown summary is proposed; full DayTrack is secondary.
4. Which non-sponsor route is integrated first after sponsor-only composition is
   reviewed, and what minimum actor-safe matter/return contract is needed?

**Risks before implementation:** unvalidated visual/game identity, 720p long-text
reflow, keyboard discovery and current-map return assumptions; stale kit/read-model
documentation versus branch implementation; absent general typed affairs IDs;
unreviewed new sponsor candidate; ongoing identity, physical input/DPI, performance
and other deferred gates. No blocker is solved by importing future content or art.

**Smallest next action after Director review:** if a thesis is selected, make one
paired static 720p/1080p wireframe of its same current sponsor situation, plus an
annotated keyboard/return walkthrough, for Director composition review. Keep full
production integration outside that step. If static review cannot settle spatial
selection/focus, propose a separate isolated non-production prototype for that exact
uncertainty and obtain its bounded authorization; this exploration builds none.

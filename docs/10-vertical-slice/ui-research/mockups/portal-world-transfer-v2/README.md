## Current Portal direction

- Preserve the existing management-game shell and overall Portal architecture where practical.
- Replace the old Four-Week Operations area with a substantial Transfer News panel.
- Upgrade Latest News into a dedicated World News editorial panel.
- At roomy widths, World News and Transfer News should form one large information zone, roughly 55–60% / 40–45% respectively.
- Keep Key Decisions as the actionable triage area.
- Keep Next Match, Season Record, and Money Snapshot as compact state summaries.
- Continue remains the strongest global action and belongs in a practical top-right command position as shown in this reference.
- Visual family: graphite / charcoal / restrained deep navy surfaces, steel/silver structure, muted brass/gold accents. Avoid making bright electric blue the dominant skin and avoid gold saturation.

## Non-canonical content in the image

Every specific name, team, player, staff member, sponsor, crest, portrait, date, score, standing, cash value, fee, status, and news headline visible in the mockup is illustrative only.

Do NOT copy sample values into production code.

## Asset and missing-art rule

Use the approved semantic assets under:
`game/Client/Assets/UI/Semantic/`

Do not invent or render character/staff/event/news imagery when an approved asset does not exist. Missing visual content should degrade gracefully to truthful text, existing deterministic identity marks, or neutral placeholders that do not impersonate a specific person/entity.

The UI must remain data-driven and valid under empty, single-item, many-item, long-name, missing-art, unknown-value, and distress states.

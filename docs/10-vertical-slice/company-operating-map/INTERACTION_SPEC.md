# Interaction and return contract

Click a scope to inspect it directly. Click a situation on its scope, in the
company inspector or in Affairs to select the affected scope and show its reason,
deadline, consequence and uncertainty. Open decision entry replaces the map
reading region with a read-only handoff document. No View → Open screen chain.

The company identity and selected path remain above all content. Entry document
identifies company scope, situation, evidence and trade-off. Sponsor flow: company
→ sponsor situation / Business & Finance → commercial commitment entry → Return.
Preparation flow: Primary Team situation → Preparation review entry → Return.
Talent and obligation use the same bounded read-only entry mechanism. No negotiation,
gameplay commitment, optimistic outcome, time advancement or authoring controls.

Tab/Shift+Tab traverse native visible controls; Up/Down move focus among scope/
situation controls within the representation; Enter/Space activate. Focus is
distinct from selected scope and situation. Switch map/list retains both IDs and
focuses the corresponding matter, or scope if none. The hidden view is not a second
selection authority. No row indices or names route navigation.

Escape/Back returns entry → exact originating selection/mode/focus/scroll; then
situation → owning scope; then scope → parent; company Back remains company.
Explicit Company context returns home. Selection never commits. Entry cannot be
opened twice or replace its origin while active. Inspector-origin selection moves
focus to its contextual entry action; returning restores that action because the
previous summary row has been replaced by the selected situation's details.

Affairs/time toggles a small chronological region with canonical TimelineEvents.
Unknown dates sort after known dates without fabricated chronology. Selecting an
affair focuses its affected scope; returning from its entry restores affair focus.
Current day and next competition checkpoint remain in the header while Affairs
is collapsed. Quiet retains normal ongoing matters; empty retains structure and
checkpoint without fabricated alerts.

Map and inspector have independent native scroll regions, focus following and
PageUp/PageDown/Home/End when the region itself is focused. Long identity wraps;
720p intentionally scrolls detailed evidence. No horizontal scroll or drag-only
navigation. Shared Stage 2 text labels, status, focus and brand resolver are used.

Replace is a presentation lifecycle seam: same-session older revision rejects;
new/load session clears old context even if IDs match. Removed scope shows an
explanation and selects nearest valid ancestor; removed situation clears entry
and retains scope. Refresh always closes entry so old evidence cannot be acted
on. There are no persistent drafts. Physical input and DPI remain unverified.

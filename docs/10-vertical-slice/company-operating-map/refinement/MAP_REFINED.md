# Refined hybrid ownership map

Persistent company identity → company root → two native container lanes:
owned portfolio on the left, shared corporate capabilities on the right.
Contains arrows indicate ownership; supports/constrains/serves labels indicate
relationships. Sponsor attaches to Business & Finance; preparation and roster
attach to Primary Team. At most two High/Critical matters receive added emphasis.

Esports and Development Discipline share one row with an explicit arrow. Both
remain independent focusable EntityLabels; company, portfolio and team retain
their own rows. The full selected path is unchanged. The bounded predicate falls
back to separate rows if the pair branches, disappears or hosts an intermediate
matter. This is not a recursive tree or a graph layout algorithm. Additional
approved divisions would need a new bounded presentation review and data bound.

Removed the map's generic title/description above its root, repeated badge panels
below already-labelled matters, and one vertical chain row. Shared shell and
inspector improvements are specified separately. Safe brand accents use the same
Stage 2 resolver. No bespoke assets or new components.

Strength: relationships remain simultaneously readable, and parallel lanes use
less vertical space than the tree in these fixtures. Weakness: the viewer reads
two lanes, several relationship labels and extra grouping surfaces; that does not
buy a shorter verified task path. Human company/ownership comprehension is unknown.

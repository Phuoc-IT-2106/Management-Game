# PRODUCT DIRECTION

Status: PROVISIONAL

This file captures the currently recommended direction from Discovery. It does not replace future approved product decisions.

---

# Strategic Center

The game should currently be explored as:

**Company-first management simulation with esports as the starting domain.**

This does not mean esports becomes unimportant.

Instead:

- esports supplies the first operational environment,
- company management supplies the main strategic decision space,
- long-term company expansion supplies future progression potential.

---

# Recommended Player Perspective

Not yet locked.

The current concept appears strongest when the player operates mainly at:

**executive + management level**

rather than performing every operational task directly.

Potential responsibility split:

## Executive Decisions
- capital allocation
- high-level budgets
- organizational structure
- expansion
- strategic priorities
- leadership appointments
- major partnerships

## Management Decisions
- roster strategy
- important hiring
- contracts
- sponsors
- preparation priorities
- department investment

## Operational Decisions
Training schedules, detailed tactics, small daily tasks, and similar work should be considered for delegation or abstraction unless they create substantial strategic value.

---

# Competitive Role

Competition should matter because it affects the company.

Possible consequences include:

- reputation
- audience
- sponsor value
- talent attraction
- financial performance
- internal expectations
- future opportunities

Winning should be important, but the project should investigate whether winning every competition must always be the dominant objective.

A strategically correct decision may sometimes sacrifice short-term competitive performance for:
- financial stability,
- future talent,
- organizational restructuring,
- long-term expansion,
- or risk reduction.

---

# Long-Term Identity

The long-term product may evolve toward:

Esports Organization
→ Multi-Team Esports Company
→ Sports / Entertainment Group
→ Broader Strategic Company

Possible future domains:
- traditional sports
- media
- advertising
- events
- merchandising
- talent management
- facilities
- partnerships
- investments

These future domains should reuse shared company systems wherever appropriate.

They should not become disconnected mini-games.

---

# What Must Be Proven First

Before broad expansion, the game must prove that a small company simulation can repeatedly create meaningful decisions.

The first proof should not depend on:
- polished graphics,
- many sports,
- huge content libraries,
- complex accounting,
- or detailed employee simulation.

The project should prove the strategic management experience first.

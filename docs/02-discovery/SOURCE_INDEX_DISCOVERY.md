# DISCOVERY SOURCE INDEX

This file explains how the supplemental Discovery sources should be used.

---

## DISCOVERY_SYNTHESIS.md
Purpose:
Compact consolidation of the Project Director review.

Use when:
A specialist needs the current high-level interpretation without reading the entire Director conversation.

Authority:
Summary / proposal source. Not a decision record.

---

## PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md
Purpose:
Captures the currently recommended product direction.

Authority:
Provisional. Requires explicit approval before becoming canonical decision.

---

## PRELIMINARY_DESIGN_PILLARS.md
Purpose:
Provides candidate design pillars for later validation.

Authority:
Proposal.

---

## RISK_REGISTER.md
Purpose:
Tracks major product and development risks identified during Discovery.

Authority:
Active project risk source.

---

## STRATEGIC_DECISIONS_PENDING.md
Purpose:
Lists the highest-impact unresolved decisions.

Authority:
Open-question source.

---

## DISCOVERY_WORKSHEET.md
Purpose:
Collect the user's answers to the remaining Discovery questions.

Authority:
Input source. Answers become decisions only after Director consolidation.

---

## PHASE0_TO_P1_PLAN.md
Purpose:
Defines the recommended process for moving from Discovery into Product Foundation.

Authority:
Process guidance.

---

## PRODUCT_SPECIALIST_HANDOFF.md
Purpose:
Bootstrap the first specialist conversation after Discovery closes.

Authority:
Draft handoff until Project Director activates it.

---

# Source Priority

When conflicts occur, use this priority:

1. Accepted entries in DECISION_LOG
2. Current PROJECT_STATE
3. Approved canonical product documents
4. Discovery sources
5. Specialist proposals
6. Chat brainstorming

Do not silently resolve conflicts.

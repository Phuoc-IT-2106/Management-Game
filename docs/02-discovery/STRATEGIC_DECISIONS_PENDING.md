Low:
Roster + staff + preparation influence abstract match resolution.

Medium:
Add strategy, meta, opponent preparation, and more detailed performance modeling.

High:

This decision strongly affects project scope.

# D-05 — Simulation Philosophy

Possible directions:

Accessible Abstraction:
Few variables, strong causal clarity.

Medium Simulation:
Meaningful depth with abstraction where detail adds little gameplay.

Heavy Simulation:
Detailed people, competition, economics, and organizational processes.

# PRELIMINARY DESIGN PILLARS

Status: PROPOSAL

These pillars are provisional until Phase 0 Discovery is completed.

---

## P1 — Strategic Trade-offs

Important actions should consume or risk something meaningful.

Possible costs:
- money
- time
- staff capacity
- information
- reputation
- flexibility
- opportunity
- risk tolerance

Good decisions should rarely be universally optimal.

---

## P2 — Interconnected Consequences

Important systems should influence more than one outcome.

Example:

Signing an expensive star may affect:
- performance
- salary structure
- team chemistry
- sponsor appeal
- expectations
- negotiation power
- future roster flexibility

Avoid isolated mechanics that only modify one number.

---

## P3 — Adaptive Organization

A strategy that works now should not automatically remain optimal forever.

The player should periodically reconsider:
- staffing
- spending
- contracts
- roster strategy
- organizational structure
- growth priorities

because the environment and company state change.

---

## P4 — Imperfect Information

The player should make informed decisions without complete omniscience.

Information quality may depend on:
- scouting
- analytics
- staff quality
- relationships
- market access
- experience

Uncertainty should create reasoning, not arbitrary punishment.

---

## P5 — Growth Has a Cost

Expansion creates:
- capability
- revenue potential
- market access

but also:
- coordination requirements
- management overhead
- financial exposure
- staffing needs
- reputation risk

Large organizations should not behave like small organizations with larger numbers.

---

## P6 — Emergent Situations Over Scripted Chores

Persistent simulation state should create problems and opportunities.

Randomness may influence circumstances, but should not replace player reasoning.

Authored events should enrich systemic situations rather than become the sole source of variety.

---

# Validation Requirement

Each pillar should later be tested against real gameplay situations.

A pillar should not remain in the project merely because it sounds desirable.

It must produce observable gameplay value.

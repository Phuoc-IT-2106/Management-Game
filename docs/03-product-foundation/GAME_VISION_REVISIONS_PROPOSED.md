# PROPOSED REVISIONS TO GAME_VISION.md

Status: PROPOSAL — requires Project Director approval

This document does not replace the canonical `GAME_VISION.md`. It proposes targeted revisions based on accepted Phase 0 direction and the Phase 1 Product Foundation.

## 1. Product Thesis — Proposed Replacement

Current thesis is broadly compatible, but it emphasizes the long-term broader company earlier than the first validated product.

**PROPOSAL**

> A PC strategy-management simulation in which the player leads a professional esports organization as an executive, balancing competitive ambition, people, capital, commercial relationships, and organizational risk. Competitive performance creates business leverage, while evolving rivals and changing conditions force the player to adapt. The long-term company may expand beyond esports only after the core management model is proven.

Rationale:

- clarifies player role;
- makes esports the active product domain rather than only one item in a broad company description;
- states the competitive-to-business relationship;
- protects narrow early implementation.

## 2. Player Experience — Proposed Additions

Add questions such as:

- What deserves executive attention right now?
- Should I intervene or trust my staff?
- Is short-term competitive strength worth the financial and strategic cost?
- How much flexibility should I preserve?
- Which commitments become dangerous if results worsen?
- When should I restructure instead of continuing the current strategy?

## 3. Add: Player Authority Principle

**PROPOSAL**

> The player operates primarily at executive and management level. Operational micromanagement should be abstracted or delegated unless it creates meaningful strategic value. Competitive control may offer optional manual depth without becoming mandatory.

## 4. Add: Competition as Business Leverage

**PROPOSAL**

> Competitive success is a major strategic asset because it can increase reputation, audience, sponsor value, talent attraction, commercial opportunity, and future organizational capability. Winning matters, but company health and long-term value remain broader objectives.

## 5. Add: Simulation Depth Principle

**PROPOSAL**

> Use medium simulation depth by default. Deepen a system only when additional detail creates meaningful decisions, useful uncertainty, emergent behavior, or long-term consequences.

## 6. Add: Failure and Recovery Principle

**PROPOSAL**

> Failure should usually create recovery gameplay before terminal game over. Financial distress may force downsizing, asset sales, worse financing, lost talent, sponsor damage, or restructuring before true insolvency ends the campaign.

## 7. Add: First Product Boundary

**PROPOSAL**

> The first vertical slice should remain centered on one company, one esports discipline, one primary team, limited staff, finance, contracts, competition, sponsorship, reputation, rival organizations, delegation, and time progression. Additional industries remain outside the first slice.

## 8. Existing Sections That Should Remain

**FACT**

The following existing concepts remain aligned with the Product Foundation:

- strategic trade-offs;
- interconnected systems;
- changing environment;
- organizational growth;
- emergent stories;
- information-based decisions;
- anti-repetition through changing state;
- long-term company expansion as a direction rather than mandatory linear progression.

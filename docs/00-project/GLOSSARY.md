# PROJECT GLOSSARY

## Company
The complete business entity controlled by the player.

## Division
A major operating branch of the company, such as an esports team, sports club, media business, or advertising unit.

## Department
An internal organizational function such as finance, scouting, marketing, HR, analytics, or legal.

## Organization
A generic entity representing a structured company, club, or competitive operator. Use carefully when "Company" is more precise.

## Esports Discipline
A specific competitive videogame in which teams compete.

## Team
A competitive roster operating inside a sports or esports division.

## Staff
Non-player personnel contributing skills, capacity, decisions, or operations.

## Resource
Anything limited that can affect decision-making, including money, time, reputation, staff capacity, information, audience, or infrastructure.

## Faucet
A process that introduces a resource into the system.

## Pool
Where a resource accumulates.

## Sink
A process that removes or consumes a resource.

## Company State
The authoritative simulation state describing the company and its owned entities.

## World State
The wider simulation state outside the player's direct company.

## Simulation Tick
A discrete advancement of simulated time.

## Vertical Slice
A small but complete end-to-end playable representation of the intended game.

## Core Loop
The recurring pattern of observing information, making decisions, advancing simulation, receiving consequences, and adapting.

## Emergent Gameplay
Gameplay situations produced by interactions between systems rather than by fixed scripts.

## Source of Truth
The canonical project document, specification, or data structure that has authority over conflicting informal information.

## Decision Log
The record of approved project-level decisions and their reasoning.

## ADR
Architecture Decision Record: a short record explaining a major technical decision and its trade-offs.

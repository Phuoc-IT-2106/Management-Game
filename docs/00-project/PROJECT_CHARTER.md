# PROJECT CHARTER

## Working Concept
A PC strategy / management / simulation game about building, operating, and expanding a modern company.

The initial playable focus is a professional esports organization. Over time, the company may expand into other business domains such as:

- traditional sports
- media and content
- advertising and sponsorship
- events
- merchandising
- talent management
- facilities
- partnerships
- investments
- other commercial ventures

The long-term fantasy is not limited to "managing an esports team." It is about building a multi-division company whose departments, people, brands, assets, markets, and strategic decisions interact.

## Core Fantasy
Start with a focused organization and gradually build a larger company.

The player should feel like an owner / executive decision-maker who:

- allocates limited resources
- hires and develops people
- builds teams and departments
- enters and exits markets
- negotiates commercial relationships
- reacts to competitors and market changes
- balances short-term survival with long-term growth
- creates synergies between business divisions
- accepts risk in exchange for opportunity
- shapes the identity and strategy of the company

## Genre
Primary:
- Management
- Business Simulation
- Strategy

Supporting:
- Resource Management
- Organizational Simulation
- Competitive Simulation
- Emergent Narrative

## Initial Scope
The first vertical slice should normally focus on:

- 1 company
- 1 esports division
- 1 primary competitive team
- a limited staff structure
- a limited market
- basic finance
- contracts
- competitive results
- sponsorship
- reputation
- time progression

This scope is a validation target, not the final game scope.

## Long-Term Direction
The company model should be expandable without requiring every possible business domain to exist in the first version.

Future divisions should ideally integrate through shared company-level systems such as:

- capital
- cash flow
- brand
- reputation
- staff capacity
- infrastructure
- audience
- commercial relationships
- risk
- information
- executive attention

## Design Goal
The game should generate changing strategic problems rather than repetitive chores.

The player should repeatedly face decisions where:

- multiple options are reasonable
- resources are constrained
- information is imperfect
- consequences may be delayed
- systems affect each other
- market conditions can change
- previous choices create new opportunities or problems

## Non-Goals for Early Development
Do not attempt to build:

- every business industry
- multiple sports at once
- a complete global economy
- highly detailed accounting
- realistic simulation of every employee
- complex 3D presentation
- large procedural content systems

until the central management loop has been validated.
